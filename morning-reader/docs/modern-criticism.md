# Modern criticism for the classics shelf

Copyrighted, so they aren't in the criticism packs. Most links are archive.org lending pages. A bought EPUB becomes a pack with `./pack file`.

## Pride and Prejudice

- Ian Watt (ed.), *Jane Austen: A Collection of Critical Essays* (1963). Has Harding's "Regulated Hatred", Mudrick and Brower; start here. https://archive.org/details/janeaustencollec00watt
- Tony Tanner, *Jane Austen* (1986). Its "Knowledge and Opinion" chapter covers how the characters come to know each other. https://archive.org/details/janeausten00tony
- Reuben Brower, "Light and Bright and Sparkling", in *The Fields of Light* (1951). https://archive.org/details/fieldsoflight0000reub
- Dorothy Van Ghent, *The English Novel: Form and Function* (1953). https://archive.org/details/englishnovelform00vang
- Marvin Mudrick, *Jane Austen: Irony as Defense and Discovery* (1952). https://archive.org/details/janeaustenironya0000mmud
- B. C. Southam (ed.), *Critical Essays on Jane Austen* (1968). Has Ryle's "Jane Austen and the Moralists". https://archive.org/details/criticalessayson0000unse_d4l5
- E. Rubinstein (ed.), *Twentieth Century Interpretations of Pride and Prejudice* (1969). https://archive.org/details/twentiethcentury00rubi
- Marilyn Butler, *Jane Austen and the War of Ideas* (1975). https://archive.org/details/janeaustenwarofi0000butl
- Alistair Duckworth, *The Improvement of the Estate* (1971). https://archive.org/details/improvementofest0000duck
- Claudia Johnson, *Jane Austen: Women, Politics, and the Novel* (1988). https://archive.org/details/janeaustenwomenp0000john
